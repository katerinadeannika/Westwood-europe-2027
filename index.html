Paris — Trip Info

Arrival
Gare du Nord — Eurostar from Brussels. Take Metro Line 4 south to Montparnasse-Bienvenüe (~15 min).
Departure
Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) — flight home to Indianapolis. RER B from Denfert-Rochereau or Metro 4 to Gare du Nord then RER B.
Metro Line 4: Gare du Nord → Montparnasse-Bienvenüe (direct, ~15 min). Stations near your lodging: Edgar Quinet (Line 6), Vavin (Line 4), Montparnasse-Bienvenüe.
